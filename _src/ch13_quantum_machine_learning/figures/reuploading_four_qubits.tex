% Four-qubit re-uploading classifier with entanglement (Perez-Salinas et al., Quantum 4, 226 (2020), Fig. 5b).
% Each layer: one compressed rotation U(theta_{q,l} + w_{q,l} o x) on every qubit, then CZ gates alternating between
% the pairs (0,1),(2,3) after odd layers and (1,2),(0,3) after even layers; no CZ after the last layer.
% Without entanglement the CZ gates are absent.
