goal: Simulate the standard quantum-information protocols end to end, with sampling and noise
---
One protocol per notebook, each derived, simulated shot by shot and then exposed to noise. Bell states and the
CHSH inequality come first, with the local-hidden-variable and Tsirelson bounds derived. Teleportation, entanglement
swapping and superdense coding use mid-circuit Bell measurements with classical feed-forward. GHZ states are
prepared, certified and degraded by local noise.

The last two notebooks turn to learning a state from measurements: quantum state tomography by linear inversion,
least squares and maximum likelihood, and classical shadows, which predict many observables from randomised
measurements without reconstructing the full density matrix.
